05 — FAQ

Quick answers

Who can use esstracker?

Employees see My Day. Admins get the full dashboard, reports, screenshots, and approvals.

How do idle gaps work?

Idle time shows as gaps on the bar. Employees click to submit offline time for admin approval.

Is this cloud or self-hosted?

Self-hosted by ESS Tracker — private, secure, under your control.

How do I get started?

Click Login with your company credentials. The desktop agent syncs activity automatically.

What does the agent track?

Applications, websites, idle periods, and optional screenshots synced to your server.
